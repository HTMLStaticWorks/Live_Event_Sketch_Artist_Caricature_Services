How to Choose Between Digital and Paper Sketches

May 14, 2026 • By Alex Rush

When planning the entertainment for your next big event, you might find yourself weighing the options between traditional paper sketches and modern digital art. Both offer a unique experience, but which is right for your crowd?

The Appeal of Paper

There is something undeniably authentic about watching an artist work with physical mediums. The scratch of the pen, the smell of the ink, the tangible piece of art you get to hold at the end. Paper sketches offer a premium, classic feel that is perfect for elegant weddings and artisan-themed parties.

The Power of Digital

Digital sketching, performed on an iPad or drawing tablet, brings a whole new level of versatility. The art can be projected live onto a large screen, meaning the entire room can watch the portrait come to life. Additionally, digital files are easily branded with corporate logos and can be instantly emailed or texted to guests for immediate social media sharing.

"The choice often comes down to the vibe of your event. For a classic romance, choose paper. For high-energy engagement, go digital."

Conclusion
