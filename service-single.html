Our Live Sketching Experience

What to expect when you book with us

When you book SketchRush, you aren't just hiring an artist; you're bringing a unique form of interactive entertainment to your event. Our artists become part of the celebration, engaging with guests while capturing their unique features with bold, confident strokes. It's a performance that results in a piece of art.

The Magic of Live Art

There is something mesmerising about watching a blank piece of paper transform into a recognizable portrait in just a few minutes. Guests love to gather around the artist's easel, cheering as they see their friends and family members appear on the page. Whether it's a digital tablet projected on a large screen or classic ink on premium paper, the energy created is infectious.

Behind the Pencil

Our artists are seasoned professionals who specialize in speed and character. At a typical event, the artist sets up in a well-lit area and begins sketching almost immediately. They work quickly—usually 3 to 5 minutes per face—ensuring that a high volume of guests can participate throughout the night. The interaction is lighthearted, fun, and never intrusive to the flow of your event.

"The artists at SketchRush didn't just draw our guests; they captured the soul of the party. Watching them work was as much a part of the entertainment as the art itself."
— Jessica M., Wedding Client

A Keepsake Like No Other

Unlike traditional party favors that often get forgotten, a custom portrait is something guests treasure. We use high-quality materials to ensure that every sketch is a durable piece of art. For digital services, guests receive a high-resolution file instantly, perfect for social media sharing. For traditional paper services, every sketch comes in a protective sleeve, ready to be framed and displayed at home.

Why Choose SketchRush?

We believe that art should be accessible and entertaining. Our mission is to turn the fleeting moments of your celebration into lasting memories. With over a decade of experience in corporate events, weddings, and private parties, we have refined our process to be seamless, professional, and endlessly creative.

What's Included

  • Professional artist with all necessary equipment
  • High-speed sketching (3-5 minutes per face)
  • Premium art materials or high-end digital tablets
  • Protective sleeves for all paper sketches
  • Instant digital delivery (for digital packages)
  • Full travel and setup logistics handled by us
