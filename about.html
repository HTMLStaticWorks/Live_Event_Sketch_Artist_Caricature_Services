Meet the Founder

SketchRush was founded with a simple idea: event entertainment shouldn't just be fun in the moment—it should leave you with a physical memory. With over a decade of experience, we've sketched thousands of faces, traveled across the country, and turned countless blank pages into masterpieces.

Behind the Studio

Where Every Sketch Begins
