Gallery FAQ

Do you provide the original sketches?

Yes! For paper sketches, guests take home the original physical artwork in a protective sleeve. For digital sketches, we send high-resolution files via email or QR code instantly.

Can you draw more than one person in a sketch?

Absolutely. We often draw couples, families, or small groups of friends together. It's a great way to capture a shared memory from your event.

What equipment do you bring?

We are fully self-sufficient. We bring our own professional lighting, drawing boards, and all necessary art supplies. All we typically need from the venue is a small footprint (approx. 5x5 ft) and access to a power outlet for digital setups.
